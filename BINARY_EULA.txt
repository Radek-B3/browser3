7. PRIVACY AND NETWORK DATA
---------------------------
The Provider operates no Browser3 telemetry or activity-reporting service. Browser3 does
not add collection of usage statistics or visited-page history.

Profiles, hardware-probe results, proxy credentials, cookies, history, and browsing data
remain on your computer below the normal Browser3 and Chromium data locations.

When a proxy is configured, Browser3 requests the proxy exit's country and time zone from
ip-api.com through that proxy. The service sees the proxy exit address, not your direct
public IP. The response contains the country code, time zone, and exit address and is
cached locally below %LOCALAPPDATA%\Browser3\cache. No request is made without a proxy.
The free endpoint uses unencrypted HTTP.

Chromium contains its own network services, such as component updates and browsing-safety
features. Their controls are available in Chromium settings. The Provider does not add a
separate reporting channel to them.

A proxy operator can observe traffic sent through its service. This is inherent to proxy
use and is independent of these terms.
