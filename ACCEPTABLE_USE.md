# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to official Browser3 binaries, hosted services, distribution channels, and support. It does not narrow or replace rights granted for MPL-2.0 source code.

Use Browser3 only for lawful purposes and only where you have authorization. Appropriate uses include privacy research, QA, compatibility testing, automation of your own accounts, and security assessment of systems you own or are permitted to test.

Do not use Browser3 to:

- access accounts, devices, networks, or data without permission;
- commit fraud, impersonation, credential theft, phishing, or payment abuse;
- distribute malware or evade security controls for harmful purposes;
- bypass access controls, rate limits, bans, or platform rules without authorization;
- conceal activity that violates applicable law or another person's rights;
- interfere with services or conduct denial-of-service activity.

Access to official support or services may be refused for activity that violates this policy. You remain responsible for complying with applicable law and the rules of services you use.

The separate [Export and Sanctions Notice](EXPORT_AND_SANCTIONS.md) applies to downloading,
supplying, re-exporting, supporting, and using Browser3. It also records the limitations of
the public GitHub distribution model.
